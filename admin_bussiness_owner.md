1.	Admin / Business Owner
The Admin / Business Owner will have complete access to the application and will be responsible for configuring the business, managing users and permissions, controlling products and inventory, monitoring sales and purchases, overseeing employees and delivery operations, and reviewing overall business performance.
The Admin / Business Owner can:
●	Register and Manage the Company
○	Register a company, firm, shop, or business.
○	Configure company details such as:
■	Company/Firm/Shop Name
■	Company Logo
■	Business Type
■	GST Number
■	PAN Number, where applicable
■	Address
■	Phone Number
■	Email Address
■	Financial Year
○	Update business information and company settings.
●	Create and Manage Users
○	Create users for different business functions.
○	Manage user profiles and contact information.
○	Activate, deactivate, or suspend users.
○	Reset user access where required.
●	Assign Roles and Permissions
○	Assign predefined roles such as:
■	Admin
■	Sales Officer
■	Delivery Partner
■	Accountant
■	Warehouse/Stock Manager
○	Create custom roles where required.
○	Configure module-level permissions such as View, Create, Edit, Delete, Approve, Export, and Download.
○	Restrict access to sensitive financial and business information.
●	Manage Products and Product Variants
○	Create and manage product categories and brands.
○	Create products and multiple product variants or sizes.
○	Configure SKU/Product Code, HSN/SAC Code, unit, GST rate, purchase price, and selling price.
○	Activate or deactivate products.
●	Example:
Water
○	250 ml
○	500 ml
○	1 Litre
●	Thums Up
○	200 ml
○	500 ml
○	1 Litre
○	2 Litres
●	Manage Stock and Inventory
○	View the centralized Product Stock Board.
○	Configure opening stock.
○	Monitor available stock in real time.
○	Manage stock received through purchases.
○	Monitor stock deducted through sales and deliveries.
○	Record stock adjustments, damaged stock, expired stock, and returns.
○	Monitor low-stock and out-of-stock products.
○	View complete stock movement history.
●	Manage Vehicle Stock and Delivery Loads
○	Assign products and quantities to Delivery Partners or delivery vehicles.
○	Record vehicle opening load.
○	Monitor products sold and delivered from vehicle stock.
○	Review returned stock at the end of the day.
○	Monitor stock variances between expected and actual returns.
○	Approve or investigate stock discrepancies.
●	Manage Purchase Invoices
○	Create, upload, and review purchase invoices.
○	View supplier and purchase information.
○	Approve purchase invoices where required.
○	Monitor the impact of approved purchases on inventory.
○	Track Paid, Partially Paid, and Unpaid purchase invoices.
●	Monitor Sales and Sales Orders
○	View sales orders created by Sales Officers, Delivery Partners, and other authorized users.
○	Monitor order status from creation to final delivery.
○	Review product quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, and customer details.
○	Approve orders or discounts where approval is required.
○	Monitor daily, monthly, yearly, and financial-year sales performance.
●	Manage Customers and Suppliers
○	Create and manage customers and suppliers.
○	View customer order and transaction history.
○	View customer outstanding balances.
○	Monitor supplier purchases and payable amounts.
○	Assign customers to Sales Officers where applicable.
●	Monitor Sales Officer Activities
○	View customers assigned to each Sales Officer.
○	Monitor customer visits and follow-ups.
○	Track sales orders created by each Sales Officer.
○	Set and monitor sales targets.
○	Review individual and team sales performance.
●	Monitor Delivery Partner Activities
○	Assign orders and deliveries to Delivery Partners.
○	Monitor delivery status.
○	View Delivered, Partially Delivered, Failed, and Rescheduled orders.
○	Monitor additional sales made during delivery.
○	Review payment collections.
○	Review daily expenses submitted by Delivery Partners.
○	Monitor vehicle loading, delivered quantities, and returned stock.
●	Monitor Payments and Expenses
○	View customer payments and supplier payments.
○	Monitor cash collected by Sales Officers and Delivery Partners.
○	Review pending cash handovers.
○	View and approve business expenses where applicable.
○	Monitor expense categories and overall expenditure.
●	Manage Employee Attendance
○	View employee daily check-in and check-out times.
○	Monitor attendance of Sales Officers, Delivery Partners, Accountants, and other staff.
○	View daily and monthly attendance reports.
○	Monitor late arrivals, absences, and working hours where applicable.
●	Access Dashboards and Business Analytics
○	View a centralized dashboard containing:
■	Today's Sales
■	Monthly Sales
■	Yearly Sales
■	Total Purchases
■	Total Customers
■	Total Products
■	Available Stock
■	Low-Stock Products
■	Pending Orders
■	Pending Deliveries
■	Customer Outstanding Amount
■	Supplier Payable Amount
■	Payment Collections
■	Business Expenses
○	View graphical trends and business performance indicators.
●	Generate and Access Reports
○	Access reports including:
■	Sales Reports
■	Purchase Reports
■	Inventory Reports
■	Stock Movement Reports
■	Customer Reports
■	Supplier Reports
■	Payment Collection Reports
■	Expense Reports
■	Outstanding Reports
■	GST Reports
■	Sales Officer Performance Reports
■	Delivery Partner Performance Reports
■	Attendance Reports
○	Filter reports by daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, financial year, or custom date range.
○	Export reports to PDF or Excel where supported.
●	Manage Notifications and Alerts
○	Receive notifications for:
■	Low stock
■	Out-of-stock products
■	Pending deliveries
■	Failed deliveries
■	Overdue customer payments
■	Pending cash handovers
■	Stock discrepancies
■	Important approval requests
●	View Audit and Activity Logs
○	Monitor important activities performed by users.
○	View who created, updated, approved, cancelled, or deleted records.
○	Track date and time of business-critical actions.
○	Review transaction and stock modification history.
●	Configure Application Settings
○	Configure company and business settings.
○	Configure invoice numbering and prefixes.
○	Configure GST and tax settings.
○	Configure payment methods.
○	Configure product units and categories.
○	Configure roles and permissions.
○	Configure notification preferences.
○	Configure financial-year settings.
Admin / Business Owner Daily Workflow
Login → Review Business Dashboard → Monitor Sales & Purchases → Review Orders & Deliveries → Monitor Inventory & Vehicle Stock → Review Payment Collections & Expenses → Monitor Staff Activities & Attendance → Review Approvals & Alerts → Analyze Reports & Business Performance
The Admin Dashboard should provide a complete real-time overview of the business, enabling the Admin / Business Owner to monitor and control sales, purchases, inventory, customers, employees, deliveries, payments, expenses, and overall business performance from a centralized platform.
