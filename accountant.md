4. Accountant
The Accountant will be responsible for managing purchase and sales invoices, recording payments and business expenses, monitoring receivables and payables, maintaining GST-related information, and generating financial and transaction reports.
The Accountant can:
●	Manage Purchase Invoices
○	Create and upload purchase invoices received from suppliers.
○	Record supplier details, invoice number, invoice date, products, quantities, purchase prices, discounts, and GST details.
○	Upload supporting invoice documents.
○	Edit or correct purchase invoices based on permissions.
○	Track invoice payment status as Paid, Partially Paid, or Unpaid.
○	Record purchase returns and adjustments.
○	Verify the impact of approved purchases on inventory.
●	Manage Sales Invoices
○	View and manage sales invoices generated against sales orders and direct sales.
○	Generate sales invoices where authorized.
○	Verify customer, product, quantity, price, discount, and GST information.
○	Track invoice status and payment status.
○	Manage sales returns, credit notes, and invoice adjustments based on permissions.
○	Print, download, or share invoices where authorized.
●	Record Customer Payments
○	Record payments received from customers.
○	Map payments against specific invoices.
○	Support full and partial payments.
○	Record payment modes such as Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, and other configured methods.
○	Track advance payments and outstanding balances.
○	Generate payment receipts where applicable.
●	Manage Supplier Payments
○	Record payments made to suppliers.
○	Map payments against purchase invoices.
○	Track supplier outstanding balances.
○	Maintain complete supplier payment history.
●	Manage Business Expenses
○	Record day-to-day business expenses.
○	Categorize expenses such as:
■	Petrol/Diesel
■	Food and Travel
■	Office Expenses
■	Rent
■	Utilities
■	Vehicle Maintenance
■	Staff Expenses
■	Delivery Expenses
■	Other Miscellaneous Expenses
○	Verify expenses submitted by Delivery Partners and other authorized users.
○	Approve, reject, or request clarification for submitted expenses based on permissions.
○	Upload and maintain bills and receipts.
●	Cash and Payment Reconciliation
○	Verify payments collected by Delivery Partners and Sales Officers.
○	Reconcile cash collected against recorded transactions.
○	Track pending cash handovers.
○	Record cash received from employees.
○	Identify and report payment discrepancies.
●	Manage Receivables and Payables
○	View customer outstanding balances.
○	Monitor overdue customer invoices.
○	View supplier payable amounts.
○	Track upcoming and overdue supplier payments.
○	Generate customer and supplier statements.
●	GST and Tax Management
○	View GST-related sales and purchase information.
○	Verify GST rates, HSN/SAC codes, and taxable values.
○	View CGST, SGST, and IGST summaries.
○	Generate GST-related transaction summaries.
○	Identify missing or incorrect GST information for correction.
○	Support GST reporting and reconciliation requirements.
●	Financial and Transaction Reports
○	Generate reports such as:
■	Daily Transaction Report
■	Sales Report
■	Purchase Report
■	Customer Outstanding Report
■	Supplier Outstanding Report
■	Payment Collection Report
■	Expense Report
■	Cash Collection Report
■	GST Summary Report
■	Sales Return Report
■	Purchase Return Report
■	Profit and Loss Summary, where applicable
●	Daily, Monthly, and Yearly Reporting
○	Generate daily financial summaries.
○	Generate monthly sales, purchase, payment, and expense reports.
○	Generate yearly and financial-year-based reports.
○	Apply custom date-range filters for transaction analysis.
●	Export and Download Reports
○	Export reports to PDF and Excel where authorized.
○	Print financial and transaction reports.
○	Download supporting transaction documents based on permissions.
●	Audit and Transaction History
○	View transaction history and modification logs where authorized.
○	Track who created, updated, approved, or cancelled a transaction.
○	Maintain traceability for financial records.
Accountant Daily Workflow
Login/Check-In → Review Purchase & Sales Invoices → Record Customer and Supplier Payments → Verify Employee Collections → Review and Approve Expenses → Reconcile Cash and Transactions → Monitor Receivables & Payables → Review GST Information → Generate Daily Financial Summary
The Accountant Dashboard should provide a summary of total sales, total purchases, payments received, payments made, customer outstanding amounts, supplier payables, pending cash handovers, expenses, overdue invoices, and GST-related summaries.
