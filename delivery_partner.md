3. Delivery Partner
The Delivery Partner will be responsible for managing assigned deliveries, creating customers and sales orders during field operations, collecting payments, recording daily expenses, managing vehicle stock, and marking attendance.
The Delivery Partner can:
●	Create and Manage Customers
○	Register new customers during delivery or field visits.
○	Update customer contact and delivery information based on permissions.
●	Create Sales Orders During Delivery
○	Create new sales orders directly while visiting a customer.
○	Add products and quantities requested by the customer.
○	Sell additional products carried in the delivery vehicle, subject to available vehicle stock.
○	Generate or submit the order for billing and further processing.
●	View and Manage Assigned Deliveries
○	View all deliveries assigned for the day.
○	View customer details, delivery address, contact information, ordered products, quantities, and payment details.
●	Update Delivery Status
○	Mark orders as:
■	Delivered
■	Partially Delivered
■	Failed
■	Rescheduled
○	Record reasons and remarks for partial, failed, or rescheduled deliveries.
○	Create a Receipt based on the delivery and payment received. ( Show the last Pending Amount and current Amount) and send to Customer Whatsapp
●	Record Delivered and Extra Product Quantities
○	Record the actual quantity delivered against the original sales order.
○	Add and deliver extra products requested by the customer during delivery.
○	Ensure additional product deliveries are recorded as sales transactions and deducted from vehicle stock.
●	Record Payment Collection
○	Collect and record customer payments where authorized.
○	Record payment mode, amount collected, and outstanding balance.
○	Supported payment modes may include Cash, UPI, Bank Transfer, Cheque, and other configured payment methods.
●	Record Daily Expenses
○	Record expenses incurred during delivery operations, including:
■	Petrol/Diesel
■	Food
■	Parking
■	Toll charges
■	Vehicle maintenance
■	Other miscellaneous expenses
○	Upload bills or receipts where applicable.
○	Expenses may be subject to Admin or Accountant approval.
●	Vehicle Stock / Load Management
○	Record products and quantities loaded into the vehicle at the beginning of the day.
○	Track products delivered or sold during the day.
○	Record remaining and returned stock at the end of the day.
○	The system should calculate:
●	Opening Vehicle Load + Additional Load − Delivered/Sold Quantity = Expected Return Stock
○	Any difference between expected and actual returned stock should be recorded as a stock variance for verification.
●	Daily Attendance
○	Record daily office check-in time.
○	Record departure time for delivery activities, if required.
○	Record return-to-office time.
○	Record final check-out time when leaving the office.
○	Maintain daily and monthly attendance history.
Delivery Partner Daily Workflow
Check-In → Vehicle Stock Loading → View Assigned Deliveries → Start Delivery → Deliver Products / Create Additional Sales Orders → Collect Payments → Record Expenses → Return to Office → Record Returned Stock → Stock Reconciliation → Check-Out
The Delivery Partner dashboard should provide a daily summary of assigned deliveries, completed deliveries, pending deliveries, total sales, payment collections, expenses, vehicle stock, returned stock, and attendance status.
