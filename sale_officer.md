2. Sales Officer
The Sales Officer will be responsible for managing assigned customers, acquiring new customers, conducting customer visits, creating sales orders, following up on sales opportunities and payments, and achieving assigned sales targets.
The Sales Officer can:
●	Create and Manage Customers
○	Register new customers during field visits.
○	Capture customer details such as business name, contact person, phone number, GST number, address, and delivery location.
○	Update customer information based on assigned permissions.
○	View complete customer sales and order history.
●	View Assigned Customers
○	View the list of customers assigned by the Admin or Sales Manager.
○	Search and filter customers based on location, category, status, and visit schedule.
○	View customer outstanding balances and previous order details where permitted.
●	Create Sales Orders
○	Create sales orders during customer visits or based on customer requests.
○	Select products and required quantities.
○	View product prices and applicable discounts.
○	Apply discounts based on authorized limits.
○	Submit sales orders for approval or directly confirm orders based on permissions.
○	View and track the status of created sales orders.
●	View Product Availability
○	View the Product Stock Board.
○	Check current product availability before creating a sales order.
○	View available product variants, sizes, and selling prices.
○	Receive notifications for low-stock or unavailable products where applicable.
●	Record Customer Visits
○	Check in when visiting a customer.
○	Record visit date and time.
○	Record the purpose of the visit.
○	Add visit notes and customer feedback.
○	Record the outcome of the visit.
○	Check out after completing the customer visit.
●	Manage Follow-Ups
○	Create follow-up activities for customers.
○	Schedule the next follow-up date and time.
○	Add follow-up notes and reminders.
○	Track pending, completed, and overdue follow-ups.
○	Receive notifications for upcoming follow-ups.
●	Track Sales Targets and Performance
○	View assigned daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly sales targets.
○	Track achieved sales against assigned targets.
○	View target completion percentage.
○	Track the number of orders created and customers acquired.
○	View individual sales performance reports.
●	Customer Outstanding and Payment Follow-Up
○	View customer outstanding balances where authorized.
○	Follow up with customers regarding pending payments.
○	Record payment follow-up remarks and commitments.
○	Notify the Admin or Accountant about payment-related updates.
●	Daily Attendance
○	Record office check-in and check-out times.
○	Maintain daily and monthly attendance history.
Sales Officer Daily Workflow
Check-In → View Assigned Customers & Follow-Ups → Plan Customer Visits → Visit Customer → Create/Update Customer → Check Product Availability → Create Sales Order → Record Visit Outcome → Schedule Follow-Up → Track Daily Sales Performance → Check-Out
The Sales Officer Dashboard should provide a daily summary of assigned customers, planned visits, completed visits, pending follow-ups, sales orders created, total sales value, new customers added, outstanding payment follow-ups, and sales target achievement.
